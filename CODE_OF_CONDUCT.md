# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TerraForge a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on the project and its users, not on personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and remove sensitive information from reports.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when needed and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized language.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Disrupting project spaces or repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and evaluate incidents
fairly.
